Booking privacy

How request details are handled.

Plain-language information about the booking form and staff dashboard.

Details collected

The booking form collects the inquiry type, customer name, contact number, preferred date and time, guest count, and any optional occasion or notes. Staff may also enter requests received through Messenger, phone, or in person, including a Messenger contact name when no phone number is available.

Why Yaelitos uses them

Yaelitos uses these details to review requests, contact customers, check availability, record replies and follow-up, and maintain booking decisions. Submitting the form creates a Pending request only. It is confirmed only after Yaelitos checks the details and replies.

Who can access them

Only approved owner and staff accounts can access booking records through the private dashboard. Staff accounts are invite-only and can be deactivated by the owner.

How long details are kept

Customer name, contact number or Messenger contact name, occasion, and free-text notes are removed 12 months after the requested date. Yaelitos may retain non-identifying details such as the date, guest count, inquiry type, status, and activity timestamps for operational records.

Correction or early removal

To ask about a submitted request or request early anonymization, contact Yaelitos through Facebook Messenger or call 0912-059-3992. Include the booking reference when available.
