About Yaelitos

Your Second Home for meals, meetings, and milestones.

Yaelitos is a welcoming Filipino comfort food restaurant with private dining, catering, and event-ready service in M'lang, North Cotabato.

The Yaelitos Experience

Comfort food in a setting guests remember.

The restaurant combines familiar Filipino flavors with interiors that feel relaxed, bright, and celebration-ready. Guests can come in for daily dining, reserve tables for groups, or inquire about the VIP Room for a more private salo-salo.

Known as “Your Second Home,” Yaelitos welcomes family meals, birthdays, surprises, catering inquiries, and private gatherings in M'lang, North Cotabato.
