# Yaelitos booking desk | Mabilis na gabay

**Open / Buksan:** `https://yaelitos.vercel.app/staff/` on your own phone or work computer. Sign in with the account the owner invited. Never share your password.

## At opening and before closing | Sa pagbukas at bago magsara

1. Open **Needs reply / Kailangang sagutin**. It includes old requests whose requested date has passed. / Kasama rito ang mga lumang request na hindi pa nasasagot.
2. Open each Pending request and check the date, time, guests, contact route, and notes. Ask the owner or manager when availability, price, or setup is uncertain. / Suriin ang petsa, oras, bilang ng bisita, contact, at notes. Magtanong sa may-ari o manager kapag hindi tiyak ang availability, presyo, o setup.
3. **Contact the customer yourself.** Call, send SMS, reply in Messenger, or speak in person. Record how you replied, then choose **Confirmed** or **Rejected**. / **Ikaw ang sasagot sa customer.** Tawag, SMS, Messenger, o personal. Itala kung paano ka sumagot bago piliin ang **Confirmed** o **Rejected**.

**Pending means a request, not a reservation. / Ang Pending ay request pa lamang, hindi kumpirmadong reservation.** Changing a status does not send a message to the customer. / Walang awtomatikong mensahe kapag binago ang status.

## Add requests received elsewhere | Idagdag ang ibang request

Use **Add request** for Messenger, phone, or walk-in inquiries. Enter the date, time, guest count, and a usable contact route. A Messenger name is allowed when there is no phone number. / Gamitin ang **Add request** para sa Messenger, tawag, o walk-in. Ilagay ang petsa, oras, bilang ng bisita, at paraan ng pakikipag-ugnayan. Puwedeng Messenger name kung walang numero.

## Owner only | Para sa may-ari lamang

In **Content**, enter approved menu details, prices, regular hours, or one announcement. **Save draft**, check it, then **Publish**. Do not guess missing facts. Use **Team** to invite or deactivate staff. / Sa **Content**, ilagay lamang ang aprubadong menu, presyo, oras, o anunsyo. **Save draft**, suriin, saka **Publish**. Huwag manghula. Sa **Team**, mag-imbita o mag-deactivate ng staff.

**End of shift / Pagtatapos:** Check Needs reply once more and sign out. Keep customer details private. / Tingnan muli ang Needs reply, mag-sign out, at panatilihing pribado ang detalye ng customer.
