Booking desk · English / Filipino

Staff quick guide / Mabilis na gabay

Open staff desk

Pending means a request, not a confirmed reservation.
Ang Pending ay request pa lamang, hindi kumpirmadong reservation. Changing a status does not send a message to the customer. / Walang awtomatikong mensahe.

At opening and before closing / Sa pagbukas at bago magsara: Open Needs reply. It includes requests whose date has passed. / Kasama ang lumang request na hindi pa nasasagot.

1 · Open the request

Check date, time, guests, contact route, and notes. Ask the owner about uncertain availability or prices.

Suriin ang petsa, oras, bisita, contact, at notes. Magtanong sa may-ari kung hindi tiyak.

2 · Reply personally

Call, send SMS, reply in Messenger, or speak in person. Use the customer’s recorded contact route.

Tumawag, mag-SMS, sumagot sa Messenger, o makipag-usap nang personal.

3 · Record the outcome

Enter how and when you informed the customer, then choose Confirmed or Rejected.

Itala kung paano at kailan sumagot bago piliin ang Confirmed o Rejected.

Messenger, phone, walk-in / Ibang request

Choose Add request. Enter the date, time, guest count, and contact route. A Messenger name is enough when no phone number is available.

Piliin ang Add request. Ilagay ang petsa, oras, bilang ng bisita, at contact. Puwedeng Messenger name kung walang numero.

Owner only / Para sa may-ari

In Content, enter approved menu details, prices, seven-day hours, or one announcement. Save draft, review, then Publish. Use Team for staff invites.

Ilagay lamang ang aprubadong detalye. Save draft, suriin, saka Publish. Sa Team ang pag-imbita ng staff.

End of shift / Pagtatapos: Check Needs reply again, sign out, and keep customer details private. / Tingnan muli ang Needs reply, mag-sign out, at panatilihing pribado ang detalye ng customer.
