# Yaelitos Staff Guide

The customer website saves each completed request as **Pending** in the private staff dashboard. Staff can also enter requests received by Messenger, phone, or in person. A Pending request is not a confirmed booking. Check **Needs reply** at opening and before closing, including older requests whose requested date has passed. Staff must check details, contact the customer, record the reply, and then record the correct status. See the one-page [`STAFF-QUICK-START.md`](STAFF-QUICK-START.md) or the printable `/staff/guide.html` page for the English/Filipino shift checklist.

## Sign In Safely

1. Open `/staff/` from an approved phone or computer.
2. Sign in with the email address invited by the owner. There is no public account registration.
3. Do not share passwords or leave the dashboard open on a public device.
4. Use **Sign out** when finished. The dashboard also locks after 30 minutes without activity.

If an account should no longer have access, the owner should deactivate it immediately from the Team section.

## Handle a Pending Request

1. Open **Needs reply** and review every Pending record, including overdue dates.
2. Open the request and check the customer name, contact, requested date and time, guest count, occasion, and notes.
3. Check availability with the owner or assigned manager.
4. Reply to the customer through Messenger, SMS, phone, or in person. The dashboard does not send a message for you.
5. Record the reply channel and time in the booking. Choose **Confirmed** or **Rejected** only after the customer has been informed. Save an internal follow-up note when another staff member needs context.

Use the statuses consistently:

- **Pending** — received but not yet finally answered.
- **Confirmed** — availability was approved and staff personally told the customer.
- **Rejected** — Yaelitos cannot accept the request and staff told the customer.
- **Completed** — the confirmed meal or gathering took place.
- **Cancelled** — the customer or Yaelitos cancelled the request.

The dashboard does not automatically message customers. Changing a status records staff activity but sends no email, SMS, Messenger message, or booking confirmation. A recorded reply is a staff attestation, not proof that a message was delivered.

## Add a Messenger, Phone, or Walk-in Request

1. Choose **Add request** and select the source: Messenger, phone, or walk-in.
2. Enter the customer name, request type, date, time, and guest count. For phone follow-up, enter a number. For a Messenger conversation without a number, enter the Messenger contact name. For a walk-in handled face to face, use the in-person route.
3. Save the request. It starts as Pending; check availability, answer the customer, record the reply, and then choose the outcome.

Do not copy full private conversations into notes. Record only details needed to handle the request. Use the reference to avoid confusing customers with similar names.

## Confirmation Checklist

- Customer name and a usable phone number, Messenger contact name, or in-person route
- Inquiry type: table, private gathering, or catering/group meal
- Requested date and time
- Number of guests
- Occasion, location, food, setup, accessibility, or other special requests
- Availability checked with the responsible person
- Only owner-approved price, deposit, cancellation, and service details shared
- Customer clearly told whether the request is **Confirmed**, **Rejected**, **Cancelled**, or still **Pending**

Do not guess prices, capacity, inclusions, availability, or policies.

## Search, Filters, and Export

- Search using a request reference, customer name, or contact number.
- Use date and status filters to narrow the booking list.
- **Needs reply** includes all Pending records, even when the requested date has passed. **Today**, **Upcoming**, and **All** are separate views.
- CSV export is owner-only and exports the current filters. Treat exported files as private customer records and delete local copies when no longer needed.

## Owner Controls

The owner can invite, deactivate, and reactivate staff; view important activity; export CSV; and anonymize customer details early when needed. Staff cannot manage other accounts or export the full list.

In **Content**, the owner can enter a draft menu with optional prices and availability, all seven regular opening days, or one announcement. Use **Save draft**, review the details, then **Publish**. Use **Unpublish** immediately when published information is no longer accurate; the public site will return to its contact fallback. Staff can read published details to answer inquiries but cannot edit or publish them. Exact prices, hours, event capacity, and package terms need the owner's approval before publication.

Customer name, phone or Messenger name, occasion, customer notes, internal follow-up notes, and reply notes are automatically anonymized 12 months after the requested date. Non-identifying booking details and activity timestamps remain for operational records.

## When to Escalate

Ask the owner or assigned manager before answering when the inquiry involves:

- A date or time conflict, a large group, or an unusual setup
- Custom menus, off-site catering, delivery limits, or special equipment
- Discounts, deposits, refunds, cancellations, or exceptions to normal policy
- Allergy, dietary, food-safety, accessibility, complaint, or urgent concerns
- A request to correct or remove stored customer details

## Updating Website Information

The owner Content tab edits the published menu, regular hours, and one announcement. For changes to the business phone, address, directions, social links, page layout, or approved photos, send the maintainer the exact correction, effective date, and owner approval. Those stable facts remain in `assets/site-config.js` or maintained page assets. Facebook promotions do not automatically appear on the website.
