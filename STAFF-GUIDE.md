# Yaelitos Staff Guide

The website has no staff login, admin dashboard, orders, or payments. Customers use it to learn about Yaelitos and prepare an inquiry, then they contact the restaurant through Facebook Messenger, SMS, or phone.

## Reply to an Inquiry

1. Open the Yaelitos Page inbox in Facebook or Meta Business Suite. SMS inquiries arrive in the restaurant phone as usual.
2. Reply as soon as practical and thank the customer.
3. Collect every detail in the checklist below.
4. Check availability with the owner or assigned manager.
5. Send one clear final reply stating whether the request is confirmed or still pending.

## Confirmation Checklist

- Customer name and working contact number
- Inquiry type: table, private gathering, or catering/group meal
- Requested date and time
- Number of guests
- Dine-in, private room, pickup, delivery, or event location, as applicable
- Food, setup, accessibility, or other special requests
- Availability checked with the responsible person
- Only owner-approved price, deposit, cancellation, and service details shared
- Customer clearly told whether the request is **confirmed** or **still pending**

Do not call an inquiry a booking until the restaurant has checked the details and replied with confirmation.

## When to Escalate

Ask the owner or assigned manager before answering when the inquiry involves:

- A date or time conflict, a large group, or an unusual setup
- Custom menus, off-site catering, delivery limits, or special equipment
- Discounts, deposits, refunds, cancellations, or exceptions to normal policy
- Allergy, dietary, food-safety, accessibility, complaint, or urgent concerns

Do not guess prices, capacity, inclusions, availability, or policies. Tell the customer you are checking and will reply once the correct information is confirmed.

## Updating Website Information

Staff do not edit the website through a dashboard. Send the website maintainer the exact correction, its effective date, and the owner's approval. Include replacement photos as original files when needed.

The maintainer updates shared business facts in `assets/site-config.js`, including the phone number, address, directions, Facebook/Messenger links, trust labels, and optional announcement. Food, event, and photo changes may also require a page or media update.

Facebook posts do not automatically appear on the website. Continue publishing day-to-day promotions on Facebook and request a website update only for information that should remain on the main site.
