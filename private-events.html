Good to know

Clear answers before you send.

Does sending the form confirm my booking?

No. The form prepares your message. Your request is confirmed only after Yaelitos replies and agrees to the details.

Why are capacity, inclusions, and prices not listed?

Those details may change. Staff will give you current, accurate information for your preferred date and type of gathering.

Can I ask about food and setup together?

Yes. Add both to the notes in one inquiry so the team can respond in the same Messenger conversation.
